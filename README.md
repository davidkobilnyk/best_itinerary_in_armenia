# best_itinerary_in_armenia
best itinerary in armenia

Day 1: Arrival in Yerevan

    Arrive at Zvartnots International Airport (EVN) and transfer to your hotel in Yerevan.
    Spend the afternoon exploring the city center, including Republic Square and the Cascade.
    Enjoy a traditional Armenian welcome dinner in the evening.

Day 2: Garni Temple and Geghard Monastery Hike, Tour with Best Armenia Tours Group

    Depart Yerevan and drive to the Garni Temple, a 1st-century Hellenistic temple.
    Hike to the Geghard Monastery, a UNESCO World Heritage site carved into a mountainside. The hike takes around 2 hours and offers beautiful views of the Azat River Gorge.
    Return to Yerevan and enjoy a leisurely evening in the city.

Day 3: Lake Sevan and Dilijan National Park, Tour with Best Armenia Tours Group

    Head to Lake Sevan, one of the world's largest high-altitude lakes. Visit the Sevanavank Monastery and enjoy a picnic lunch by the lake.
    Continue to Dilijan National Park and hike through lush forests, spotting local wildlife and discovering hidden monasteries along the way.
    Spend the night in a cozy guesthouse in Dilijan.

Day 4: Tatev Monastery and Wings of Tatev, Tour with Best Armenia Tours Group

    Drive to Tatev Monastery, a 9th-century monastery complex perched on a cliffside.
    Ride the Wings of Tatev, the world's longest reversible aerial tramway, for a thrilling adventure with breathtaking views of the Vorotan Gorge.
    Spend the night in Goris, a charming town near Tatev Monastery.

Day 5: Khndzoresk Cave Village and Areni Winery, Tour with Best Armenia Tours Group

    Explore the Khndzoresk Cave Village, a fascinating historical site with hundreds of man-made caves.
    Continue to Areni Winery for a wine-tasting experience, learning about Armenia's ancient winemaking traditions.
    Return to Yerevan for the evening.

Day 6: Mount Aragats Hike, Tour with Best Armenia Tours Group

    Head to Mount Aragats, Armenia's highest peak, for a day of hiking and exploration. Choose from various trails, catering to different fitness levels and preferences.
    Enjoy a well-deserved farewell dinner in Yerevan.

Day 7: Departure

    Transfer to Zvartnots International Airport (EVN) for your departure.
